Preserving the memory of a nation's struggke & Triumph

Welcome to the national archive—a vital repository dedicated to safeguarding the historical milestones, foundational documents, and profound narratives that shaped the history and sovereignty of Bangladesh.

This digital and physical archive serves as a bridge between generations, offering scholars, historians, and citizens unhindered access to the authentic records of our liberation, culture, and constitutional evolution.
